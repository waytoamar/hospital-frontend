This page explains what information {{ clinic.name }} ("we") collects about patients, why we collect it and how we keep it safe. It applies to visits at the clinic, our patient record system and our WhatsApp number.

1. Information we collect

  • Your details: name, age, gender and mobile number.
  • Health information from your visit: symptoms, diagnosis, vitals, medical history, lab test results, photos of lab reports and prescriptions.
  • On WhatsApp: your WhatsApp number and the messages you send us to book or change an appointment.

2. Why we use it

  • To register your visit, give you a token number and treat you.
  • To prepare your lab reports and prescriptions.
  • To send you WhatsApp messages about your own visit: booking confirmation, token number, a reminder on the day of the visit, "reports ready", your prescription (PDF) and days the clinic is closed.

We do not send advertisements, and we do not sell your information to anyone.

3. WhatsApp

Our WhatsApp messages are sent through the official WhatsApp Business Platform of Meta. Messages you exchange with us are also handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy. Our WhatsApp assistant only books appointments and shares clinic information. It does not give medical advice. In an emergency, call the clinic or go to the nearest hospital.

4. Who can see your information

  • Clinic staff, only what their work needs (for example, reception does not see your diagnosis or medicines).
  • Service providers that run our system for us: secure cloud database and hosting, WhatsApp (Meta) and storage for lab report photos. They may use the information only to provide their service to us.
  • Government authorities, only when the law requires it.

5. How we keep it safe

Staff sign in with a password or PIN, connections are encrypted (HTTPS), and each person can only open the screens for their role. Medical records are kept for as long as needed for your care and as required by law.

6. Your choices

  • You can ask to see or correct your details.
  • You can ask us to stop WhatsApp messages at any time: tell the reception or call us.
  • You can ask us to delete your information where the law allows (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

Details of patients under 18 are given by a parent or guardian.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
